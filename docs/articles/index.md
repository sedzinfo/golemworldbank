# Articles

### Charts in R

- [Indicator charts in
  R](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemworldbank/articles/golemworldbank.md):

  Find countries and World Bank indicators in R and compare them with
  interactive plotly line charts, the same as the golemworldbank app.

- [Animated
  charts](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemworldbank/articles/animated.md):

  Animated World Bank charts in R that play through the years: a
  population pyramid, a bar chart race, a bubble chart and a map.

- [Changes and the indicator
  index](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemworldbank/articles/tables.md):

  Which World Bank indicators rose and fell for a country between two
  years, and the index of every indicator with its definition, as tables
  in R.
